About

The Quiet Kettle started as a page of notes taped inside a kitchen cupboard: water temperatures, steep times, and which tins needed drinking first. It grew into a small site because friends kept asking for the same answers.

The guides here aim to be short, accurate and free of jargon. There are no affiliate links and nothing to buy — just enough information to make a better cup tomorrow morning than you made today.

If something here is wrong or unclear, the contact page explains how to say so.
