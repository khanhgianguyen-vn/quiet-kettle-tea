Brewing guide

Three numbers decide most cups: water temperature, steep time and how much leaf you use. Get them roughly right and almost any decent tea will taste good.

TeaWaterFirst steepLeaf per 250 ml
Green70–80 °C1–2 min2–3 g
White80–90 °C2–4 min3–4 g
Oolong85–95 °C1–3 min3–5 g
Black90–98 °C3–5 min2–3 g

Without a thermometer

Boil the kettle, then wait: about two minutes with the lid off for oolong, four or five for white, and six or more for green. Pouring boiling water into a cool jug first also drops the temperature by roughly ten degrees.

Water

Filtered or soft water lets the tea's flavour come through. Very hard water can make tea flat and leave a film on the surface.
