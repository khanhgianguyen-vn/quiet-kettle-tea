Storing tea

Tea goes stale through five things: light, air, heat, moisture and strong smells. Keep it away from all five and most teas will stay good for many months.

Containers

Use opaque, airtight tins or pouches. Glass jars look lovely on a shelf but let light in. Keep tea well away from the stove, the spice rack and the coffee.

By family

  • Green tea — the most fragile. Drink within a few months of opening; unopened packs can go in the fridge if sealed tightly.
  • Light oolong — similar to green; buy in small amounts.
  • Black and roasted oolong — will keep a year or more.
  • Pressed white tea and pu-erh — can be aged deliberately with some airflow and stable humidity.
