Contact

Corrections, questions and suggestions for teas worth writing about are all welcome.

The simplest way to reach us is to open an issue on the site's public repository. Please include the page you are writing about and, for corrections, a source if you have one.

We read everything, though replies can take a little while — usually about as long as a good pot of oolong.
