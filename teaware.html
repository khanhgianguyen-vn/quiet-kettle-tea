Teaware

You can brew excellent tea with a mug and a strainer. Everything else is about convenience or about getting more out of particular teas.

The essentials

  • A teapot with room for the leaf to open — cramped infusers make weak tea.
  • A fine strainer — for broken-leaf teas that slip through a pot's spout holes.
  • A kitchen scale — grams are far more reliable than teaspoons.

Nice to have

  • A gaiwan — a lidded bowl ideal for short, repeated oolong and white tea infusions.
  • A variable-temperature kettle — the single biggest upgrade for green tea drinkers.
  • A glass pitcher — decant the tea so it stops steeping.
